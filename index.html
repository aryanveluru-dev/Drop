Couponly

Your scannable card & coupon wallet — plus where to get real in-store savings.

Store the barcodes you legitimately have — above all your loyalty cards (Target Circle, CVS ExtraCare, Kroger Plus…) and real printed-coupon barcodes. The app renders them full-screen in the exact symbology the register expects (Code 128, UPC-A, EAN-13) so they scan first try. Works offline at the checkout line. Reminder: a barcode only rings up a discount if its number is a real coupon or loyalty ID the store already recognizes — this shows your codes, it can't invent working ones.
Live coupon codes pulled from Coupons.com plus each store's official savings program. We link every offer to its source — we never copy a barcode (that's fraud and wouldn't clear). Code at checkout → reveal & enter it online. Clip to card → clip in the store app, then scan your loyalty barcode in-store and it auto-applies. Print your own → the printed barcode scans at checkout. Offers rotate often — verify at the source.
